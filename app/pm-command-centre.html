Guided workflows

Follow a short playbook instead of learning every option at once.

Create and activate an initiative
  1. Define a measurable outcome and accountable owner.
  2. Add dates and only confirmed dependency IDs.
  3. Use the scoring guide to assess value, urgency, confidence, capacity and effort.
  4. Save as Draft while shaping the work; move to Active / On track when committed.
Prioritise the portfolio
  1. Confirm mandatory obligations and deadlines first.
  2. Review confidence and capacity—not just value.
  3. Resolve blocked or unready dependencies.
  4. Use the Backlog rank as a decision aid, then document trade-offs.
Run a risk and control review
  1. Describe cause, event and impact clearly.
  2. Assign an owner and preventive or detective control.
  3. Record evidence and the next action.
  4. Escalate high-rated risks and connect them to delivery decisions.
Prepare the weekly executive update
  1. Update progress, health, risks and decisions.
  2. Check the top priorities and urgent commitments on Overview.
  3. Review the generated report for accuracy.
  4. Add manual commentary only for verified context not present in the records.
